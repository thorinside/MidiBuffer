# Vital receiver smoke evidence

On macOS arm64, `make test` used installed Vital AudioUnit 1.5.5 (component aumu/Vita/Tyte, version 66821). The test created its own virtual CoreMIDI source, input, output and destination, connected only its input to its own source, and sent `90 3c 64` through those endpoints to `MusicDeviceMIDIEvent`. It rendered 16,384 frames at 44,100 Hz offline with `AudioUnitRender`; the observed peak was 0.357632101. No physical MIDI or audio device was selected. This is a direct note smoke, not an MPE interoperability comparison.

The checked-in AU class-state fixture `vital-smoke-state.plist` has SHA-256 `e73ac705608e5cec4ec728aecca12c4d2ab0317fd3046fa1f73f8fa5b5d02d01`. It is an untitled init patch, MPE disabled, pitch bend range two semitones, and all modulation slots empty (including pressure and timbre mappings). The smoke restores it and captures the resulting class state in `build/vital-smoke-state.plist`; `verify_vital_state.py` checks the patch and mapping readback. Vital regenerates the White Noise sample on restore, so its sample bytes are excluded from equality; the remaining saved state is compared. The later MPE comparison must configure and capture its own relevant mappings and receiver capabilities.
